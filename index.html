How I work

Planning, Import & Manage.

🔄

S&OP cycle

Every month, sales, supply and production agree one plan.

S&OPmonthly cycle
  1. 01
    Gather the numbers

    Sales history, stock on hand and open orders for every SKU.

  2. 02
    Demand review

    Check the forecast against linear regression and exponential smoothing.

  3. 03
    Supply review

    Turn demand into raw material and packaging needs from the BOMs, with lead times and MOQs.

  4. 04
    Balance the gaps

    Spot shortages early and line up suppliers with RFQs.

  5. 05
    Agree the plan

    One plan everyone follows, then POs go out.

🚢

Import journey

How raw material and packaging get from the sea to the line.

Simple drawing, not to scale
  1. 🏦
    LC opened

    Supplier, price and Incoterms agreed; the bank opens the Letter of Credit.

  2. 🚢
    At sea

    Goods are shipped and the Bill of Lading (B/L) is issued.

  3. ⚓
    Chattogram port

    Customs clearance, with an eye on demurrage days.

  4. 🚚
    On the road

    Trucks bring the goods to the warehouse.

  5. 🏭
    Into production

    Material is ready on the line, on time.
